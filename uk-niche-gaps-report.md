# UK Niche-Gap Report — 30 verticals researched & adversarially verified

> Workflow: 64 agents, ~2M tokens. Each niche got a live-web research pass + a skeptic that tried to
> *refute* it. Pain confirmed real on **25 of 30**. Honest headline below.

---

## ⚠️ THE HONEST HEADLINE

**"No competition in the UK" is mostly a myth.** The adversarial verifier found existing software in
*almost every* niche — often **hidden UK-native incumbents** the first pass missed (Field Ascend for
drainage, Arb Pro & EZYTREEV for arborists, Smart Scaffolder for scaffolding, Hippovibe for livery yards,
Nutridata for butcher allergen labelling, Dentally for dental). These sleepy markets have already been
found by other indie founders.

**But the research surfaced something more useful: a repeatable WINNING WEDGE.** The incumbents are
consistently **(a) US-built and blind to UK compliance, (b) expensive per-seat, (c) clunky/old.** The gap
isn't "unserved" — it's **"an AI-native, UK-compliance-led, flat-priced challenger in a niche with a hard
regulatory catalyst."** That pattern repeats across the strongest candidates.

## 🔎 THE TOP CANDIDATES IN DETAIL

### 1. Septic / off-mains drainage tankers — *highest underserved score (7/10)*
- **Pain (confirmed):** operators run on "spreadsheets, whiteboards and sticky notes → double-bookings and missed recurring jobs"; manual routing causes backtracking/missed disposal stops. Asset-heavy licensed operators.
- **What exists:** strong US tools (Tank Track ~$149/truck, ServiceCore) that ignore UK rules; one UK generic (**Field Ascend** — the main red flag, does "drainage" but not septic-specialised).
- **Wedge:** UK-specific **route optimisation + EA Upper-Tier waste-carrier compliance + automatic recurring-empty reminders**, flat-priced. Hard regulatory catalyst (septic General Binding Rules).
- **Money:** £55–95/truck; a 3–6 truck firm = £120–300/mo. **Medium-high willingness to pay** (these are real asset businesses). ~1,500–3,000 firms → a few hundred customers = £25–60k MRR.
- **Distribution:** UKWTA / British Water member directories.
- **Risk:** Field Ascend already adjacent; must out-specialise on septic + compliance.

### 2. Dental admin-automation layer — *biggest willingness to pay (£99–199/mo)*
- **Pain (confirmed, quantified):** practices average **1,061 missed appointments/year (~81 lost dentist-hours)**; staff spend **40+ hrs/month** on repetitive admin; CQC/GDPR compliance run on spreadsheets.
- **What exists:** strong PMS incumbents (SOE Exact, Dentally, Carestack) — but they're the *record system*, not the *admin-automation layer*.
- **Wedge:** sit *alongside* the PMS and automate **recall/DNA recovery, FP17 claim chasing, plan reconciliation, CQC prep.** Real ROI (recovered missed appts pay for it many times over).
- **Money:** ~8–10k independent practices *with budgets*; £99–199/mo → just 200 practices = £20–40k MRR.
- **Risk:** not underserved at the *core*; must be a sharp "recover lost revenue" wedge, not a PMS.

### 3. Tree surgeons / arborists — *real pain, clean AI-compliance wedge*
- **Pain (confirmed):** ArboristSite forum — existing tools "too expensive," Jobber "cost more than I wanted." **£20,000 strict-liability fines** for working a protected (TPO) tree unknowingly; 6–8 week consent waits to track.
- **What exists:** US tools + UK **Arb Pro** and **EZYTREEV** (already does TPO) — so the core is served.
- **Wedge:** narrow — **AI-driven TPO/consent tracking + RAMS** + flat pricing undercutting per-seat. Only enter on the compliance angle, not as a generic "arborist CRM."

---

## ❌ RULED OUT (already well served / too small / too cheap)
- **Driving instructors** — multiple apps already; £12–18 ceiling, low willingness to pay.
- **Dry-stone walling / heritage masonry** — TAM under ~1,500 real businesses; mostly hobbyists.
- **Chimney sweeps, farriers, mobile groomers, tattoo studios, music tutors** — generic field-service tools suffice; price-sensitive sole traders; tiny ACV.
- **Livery yards, butchers, scaffolding** — real pain but crowded with hidden incumbents and price-sensitive; only enter with a very sharp compliance wedge.

---

## ✅ THE DECISIVE RECOMMENDATION

Don't chase "empty." Chase the **wedge**: *AI-native + UK-compliance-led + flat-priced, in a niche with a regulatory catalyst and real businesses that pay.* Two best fits:

1. **Septic / off-mains drainage** — the most *underserved* with payers and a hard compliance catalyst. Best "gap" play, if we can out-specialise Field Ascend.
2. **Dental admin-recovery layer** — the most *money* (£99–199/mo, quantified ROI), if we accept it's a wedge beside the PMS, not a greenfield.

**My pick to validate first: septic/off-mains drainage** — highest underserved score, asset-heavy operators who pay, clear compliance hook, and only one adjacent UK incumbent to beat.
