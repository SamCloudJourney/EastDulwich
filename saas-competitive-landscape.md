## 🎯 WHAT THEY HAVE vs WHAT THEY DON'T

**What incumbents HAVE (our barriers to beat):**
- Large established communities = trust, SEO dominance, brand.
- Broad bookmaker coverage (100+).
- Mature feature sets (Kelly staking, bet tracking, CLV).

**What they DON'T (our wedges):** ranked by exploitability —
1. **Trust & billing honesty** — the #1 complaint everywhere: hidden fees, charged-after-cancel, "guarantee traps", terrible support (OddsJam 2.9★). *A transparent, no-tricks brand wins disproportionately here.*
2. **Modern UX, mobile-first** — RebelBetting "difficult to navigate"; OddsMonkey "buggy", bad on phone, stripped-down rewrite. *Clean, fast, mobile-native is wide open.*
3. **Price** — incumbents £60–£800/mo; the market is sliding to £5–£19. *Undercut with a fair price (~£15–25) and over-deliver.*
4. **AI-native** — the structural shift. Incumbents bolt AI on; challengers are AI-first (conversational, custom models). *We're built by frontier AI from day one — natural fit.*
5. **All-in-one** — users currently juggle separate tools for value, arb, and matched betting. *One unified scanner is a real differentiator.*
6. **Accuracy/speed** — OddsJam dinged for inaccurate lines; Bet Hero won on sub-second speed. *Accuracy + verified CLV transparency is a trust signal.*

## ⚠️ Risks / honest cautions
- **Incumbent moats** are real: SEO + brand + community take time to overcome → content/track-record grind for 6–12 months.
- **Bet Hero** is the modern incumbent to watch — already fast + feature-rich.
- **The AI-challenger wave** means we're not the only "AI-native cheap" entrant — differentiation must be UK-focus + trust + all-in-one, not just "AI".
